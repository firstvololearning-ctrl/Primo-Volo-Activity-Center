
# Primo Volo Evidence Map

**Status:** Working internal research document  
**Purpose:** Map Primo Volo instructional design features to relevant second-language acquisition research, identify defensible claims, and use the evidence to guide future development.

> Primo Volo should be described as **research-informed**, not research-proven.  
> The research below supports instructional principles and design components. It does not establish the efficacy of Primo Volo as a program.

---

## Current Primo Volo Instructional Progression

**Understanding & Recognition → Guided Practice → Sentence Building → Active Recall → Independent Production**

This is a Primo Volo instructional framework. Research supports many of the individual instructional moves within this progression, but the exact five-stage sequence has not itself been experimentally validated.

---

# Evidence Map

## 1. Pictures + Clear Meaning Support

### What Primo Volo Does

New vocabulary is introduced with combinations of:

- clear visuals
- written Italian
- spoken Italian/audio
- contextual meaning support
- modeled language

### What the Research Supports

Zhang, Sala, & Gobet (2025) conducted a meta-analysis of multimedia conditions in second-language vocabulary learning.

Combining **L1 meaning information with pictures** produced stronger immediate vocabulary learning than using pictures alone or L1 meaning alone.

### Relationship to Primo

**Directly relevant**, although the study does not evaluate Primo Volo.

### Safe Claim

> Primo Volo uses visual and meaning supports consistent with research on multimedia second-language vocabulary learning.

### Implications for Primo

- Continue pairing new vocabulary with clear visuals and audio.
- Do not assume that picture-only support is always best.
- Strategic English/gloss support may be useful for true beginners.
- Meaning should be established clearly before increasingly independent retrieval is expected.

### Core Source

Zhang, X., Sala, G., & Gobet, F. (2025).  
*Effectiveness of L1 and pictures in multimedia conditions on learning second-language vocabulary: A meta-analysis.*

Full text:  
https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/3191374/

## 2. Meaning-Focused Input

### What Primo Volo Does

Students encounter vocabulary and language through:

- pictures
- audio
- modeled phrases
- scenes
- short readings
- cultural texts
- poems
- meaningful contexts

Rather than teaching vocabulary only as isolated translation pairs, Primo repeatedly places language in meaningful contexts.

### What the Research Supports

Webb, Uchihara, & Yanagisawa (2023) synthesized research on incidental L2 vocabulary learning from meaning-focused input, including:

- reading
- listening
- viewing
- reading while listening

Vocabulary can be acquired through meaning-focused exposure, but only a portion of encountered words is typically learned.

Exposure alone therefore does not guarantee acquisition.

### Relationship to Primo

**Strong rationale** for meaningful input and for following input with deliberate practice.

### Safe Claim

> Meaning-focused exposure can contribute to vocabulary learning, while repeated opportunities to practice and retrieve language remain important.

### Implications for Primo

Continue:

**encounter → understand → practice → retrieve → use**

Do not rely on exposure alone.

### Core Source

Webb, S., Uchihara, T., & Yanagisawa, A. (2023).  
*How effective is second language incidental vocabulary learning? A meta-analysis.*

Full text:  
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language-teaching/article/how-effective-is-second-language-incidental-vocabulary-learning-a-metaanalysis/E38E3468FD2090B1FA3051051DE8E70C

## 3. Recognition → Retrieval → Production

### What Primo Volo Does

Primo intentionally moves students beyond recognizing vocabulary.

The broader progression is:

**Understanding & Recognition → Guided Practice → Sentence Building → Active Recall → Independent Production**

Learners may first identify or match language and later be asked to:

- recall it
- complete language
- build sentences
- write it
- say it
- interact with another learner

### What the Research Supports

Second-language vocabulary research distinguishes between different levels of knowledge and different demands on retrieval.

Recognition is generally less demanding than independently retrieving and producing language.

Intentional vocabulary-learning activities can produce substantial learning, and retrieval practice can strengthen later access to learned vocabulary.

### Relationship to Primo

**Strongly aligned**, but the exact Primo progression has not been independently tested.

### Safe Claim

> Primo Volo intentionally moves learners beyond recognition by providing repeated opportunities to retrieve and produce familiar language with increasing independence.

### Implications for Primo

Where possible, distinguish between:

- recognition
- receptive retrieval
- productive retrieval
- supported production
- independent production

Do not treat every correct response as equivalent evidence of learning.

### Core Source

Webb, S., Yanagisawa, A., & Uchihara, T. (2020).  
Research synthesis on intentional L2 vocabulary-learning activities.

https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/modl.12671
